# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

We are committed to a professional, inclusive, and harassment-free project.
Contributors must communicate constructively, respect differing viewpoints,
accept responsibility for mistakes, and focus feedback on the work rather
than the person.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual
attention, deliberate intimidation, publishing another person's private
information, or sustained disruption of project discussions.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions and may
temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates
these standards. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository
maintainers through their Gitee profiles. Reports will be handled as
confidentially as practical, and retaliation against reporters is prohibited.

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project in
public spaces.
